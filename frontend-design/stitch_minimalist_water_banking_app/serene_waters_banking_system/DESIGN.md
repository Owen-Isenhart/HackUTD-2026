## Brand & Style

This design system translates the grounding equilibrium of a mountain-lake landscape into a tranquil, high-trust digital banking experience. It is tailored for discerning individuals and wealth stewards who seek mental clarity, deliberate control, and peace of mind in their personal finances. Rather than the frenetic, neon-tinted energy common to speculative fintech platforms, this interface feels contemplative, stable, and deeply rooted.

The visual direction merges **Minimalism** with subtle **Tactile Tonal Layering** and aquatic **Glassmorphism**. Layers of information stack like distant mountain silhouettes and water planes, organizing balance sheets, spending trends, and investment portfolios into clear visual strata. Micro-interactions move with the gentle inertia of still water—calm, fluid, and predictable—instilling profound confidence during high-stakes financial operations.

## Colors

The palette directly mirrors the horizontal strata of the reference illustration: misty pale skies, layered mountain ridges, and serene body-of-water gradients.

### Foundation & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#FBF9F5`):** Warm alabaster beige that reduces screen glare and imparts organic warmth.
- **Surface Muted (`#F5F1E9`):** Soft cream for grouped content panels, segmented controls, and secondary backings.
- **Surface Pure (`#FFFFFF`):** Crisp white for high-focus financial cards, modal drawers, and interactive inputs.

### Typography & Structure
- **Deep Slate Canvas Ink (`#1A2E35`):** Grounded charcoal-slate derived from the darkest mountain silhouette; used for headlines, account balances, and key metrics.
- **Mid Slate Charcoal (`#243B42`):** Body typography, secondary metadata, and structural dividers.
- **Mist Grey (`#8E9E9F`):** Tertiary timestamps, chart gridlines, and subtle disabled states.

### Aquatic Accents & Wayfinding
- **Lake Teal (`#2A6F7C`):** Primary action color, active tab indicators, and progress tracks.
- **Deep Marine (`#18454E`):** Pressed states and high-emphasis chips.
- **Shallow Water Teal (`#529DA8`):** Micro-chart gradient fills and interactive hover states.
- **Mist Azure (`#D3E5E6`):** Soft tinted backgrounds for tags and active pill indicators.

### Financial Indicators
- **Credits & Inflows (`#2E6B52`):** Forest moss green offering clear positive reinforcement without jarring saturation. Background wash: `#EBF5F0`.
- **Debits & Obligations (`#A74738`):** Refined terracotta rust communicating debt, expense, or warnings with quiet authority. Background wash: `#FCEEEB`.

### Translucent Elements
- **Water-Themed Nav Surface:** `rgba(245, 248, 248, 0.90)` backed with a `16px` backdrop-blur and a gentle inner teal glow `rgba(42, 111, 124, 0.08)`.

## Typography

**Manrope** is selected across all roles for its balanced geometric construction, open apertures, and modern warmth. It delivers high legibility when rendering dense financial data, decimals, and balances.

### Numerical Representation
All financial tallies, transaction lists, and portfolio balances must leverage OpenType tabular figures (`tnum`) to preserve clean vertical alignment across multiple decimal columns.

### Hierarchy & Restraint
- Primary balance displays utilize `display` and `display-mobile` with relaxed negative tracking for an architectural, premium appearance.
- Secondary descriptions and status subtitles remain light and breathable, avoiding aggressive bolds in small body text to keep screens serene.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors the broad, horizontal spans of the landscape reference. It uses structured horizontal strata rather than cramped, boxy tiles to let monetary figures breathe.

### Grid Model
- **Mobile (up to 599px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Primary views flow vertically as stacked horizontal cards.
- **Tablet (600px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Content splits into primary balance overview (5 cols) and quick actions / recent activity (3 cols).
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout capped at `1280px` max-width with `2.5rem` outer margins. Left panel controls navigation and accounts, center displays rich trends, and right shows transaction ledgers.

### Density & Breathing Room
A strict 4px base increment governs layout rhythm. Financial cards use `space-lg` internal padding to prevent numeric data from feeling constrained. Bottom scroll areas must enforce a `88px` bottom padding offset to prevent content collision with the floating frosted water navigation bar.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tonal surface layering and diffused aquatic shadows, creating the illusion of smooth stones settled in shallow water.

### Depth Levels
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Ground color `#FBF9F5`. No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Inset / Panels):** `#F5F1E9` background with a subtle border (`1px solid rgba(26, 46, 53, 0.05)`). Used for account group containers and transaction filters.
- **Level 2 (Cards & Tiles):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface lifted by an ambient tinted shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(26, 46, 53, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(42, 111, 124, 0.03);`
  Bounded by a hairline border: `1px solid rgba(26, 46, 53, 0.06)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Bottom Sheets & Popovers):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` floating with:
  `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -4px rgba(26, 46, 53, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(42, 111, 124, 0.06);`
- **Frosted Glass Floating Navbar:** Elevated via `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(140%)` over `rgba(245, 248, 248, 0.90)` with a top boundary line of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.60)` and an under-glow drop shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 -4px 24px 0 rgba(26, 46, 53, 0.05);`

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a roundedness factor of **2 (Rounded)**, referencing pebbles worn smooth by lake currents.

- **Primary Interactive Elements:** Buttons, search fields, and transaction rows feature `0.75rem` (`12px`) radii.
- **Container Cards & Dashboards:** Use `1rem` (`16px`) up to `1.25rem` (`20px`) on desktop for outer boundaries.
- **Floating Water Bar & Badges:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) to emphasize fluidity and touch accessibility.
- **Micro-charts:** Line caps, bar heads, and tooltip flags always employ smooth, organic radii (`4px`–`8px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#1A2E35` (Deep Slate) with `#FFFFFF` text. Hover transitions to `#2A6F7C` (Lake Teal). Padding: `12px 24px`, radius `12px`.
- **Secondary / Action:** `#E8F1F2` background with `#2A6F7C` text. Smooth hover scaling with `rgba(42, 111, 124, 0.08)` underlay.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background, `#243B42` text with `#F5F1E9` hover fill.

### Cards & Account Tiles
- **Balance Card:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid rgba(26, 46, 53, 0.06)`, `16px` border-radius. Highlights include small horizontal mist-toned tags for account type (e.g., "Checking •• 4920") and large figures in deep slate.
- **Savings / Horizon Card:** Soft vertical gradient transitioning from `#F5F1E9` to `#E6EFF0` with a subtle lake-teal progress tracker.

### Chips & Segmented Controls
- **Filter Chips:** `#F5F1E9` pill containers with `#243B42` text. When selected, adopts `#2A6F7C` fill with clean white text and no border.
- **Category Tags:** Tinted mist fills (`rgba(42, 111, 124, 0.08)`) with compact `11px` uppercase typography.

### Transaction UI & Lists
- **Row Architecture:** Minimalist horizontal rows separated by `1px solid rgba(26, 46, 53, 0.04)`.
- **Category Glyph Avatar:** Circular `40px` pill with low-saturation backgrounds (e.g., `#EBF1F2` for travel, `#F2ECE9` for groceries) framing slate iconography.
- **Indicators:**
  - *Income / Deposit:* Tagged with a moss green prefix: `+ $1,420.00` (`#2E6B52`).
  - *Debits / Subscriptions:* Tagged with a terracotta rust prefix: `- $48.20` (`#A74738`).

### Micro-charts & Visualizers
- **Cash Flow Area Chart:** Single line path styled in Lake Teal (`#2A6F7C`, `2.5px` stroke weight) with an area fade gradient dropping from `rgba(42, 111, 124, 0.22)` down to `rgba(245, 241, 233, 0.0)`.
- **Bar Trends:** Muted slate-blue bars (`#7BA1A8`) with the active/current period highlighted in vibrant `#2A6F7C`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Crisp white interior, `1px solid rgba(26, 46, 53, 0.12)`, `12px` radius. Focus ring glows with a 3px soft tint: `0 0 0 3px rgba(42, 111, 124, 0.18)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Curved forms in `#1A2E35` with crisp white check marks when active.

### Floating Water Navbar
- Centered dock-style bottom bar floating `16px` above the viewport floor.
- `rgba(245, 248, 248, 0.90)` frosted surface with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`.
- Icons use serene slate `#8E9E9F` for idle states, transitioning to `#2A6F7C` with a soft bottom indicator dot when active.
